# Project Design Template

This is a cross-layer authoring workspace, not another canonical story, world or state schema.
Use the relevant sections at the depth needed for the current output. Keep unknown, undecided,
inapplicable, intentional absence and an unexamined blank distinct. An empty narrative table
alone does not tell those states apart. Do not invent decisions to finish a form.

## Scope and request

- **requested_output**:
  <!-- What is being made now and what this note governs; no assumed novel, cast or image. -->
- **medium_and_extent**:
  <!-- Declared medium, scope, audience use and completion boundary; note unsupported serializers. -->
- **entry_point**:
  <!-- Existing premise, agent, setting, mechanism, event, theme, form or other starting material. -->
- **anchors_and_exclusions**:
  <!-- Preserve actual user requirements and adopted decisions, with their sources. -->
- **delegated_space**:
  <!-- What may be proposed without further clarification; delegation is not adoption. -->

## Experience, themes and form

- **audience_experience_or_inquiry**:
  <!-- What to notice, feel or question, without requiring a message or answer. -->
- **theme_status_and_scope**:
  <!-- Explicit, implicit, emergent, plural, conflicting, deferred or intentionally unused. -->
- **genre_conventions_and_departures**:
  <!-- Chosen conventions and boundaries; genre labels do not manufacture world facts. -->
- **expression_and_transitions**:
  <!-- Language, tone, visual grammar, rhythm and intended shifts, at the relevant scope. -->
- **organization_and_focus**:
  <!-- What organizes attention, presentation order and viewpoint; no required protagonist count. -->

## Authorial intent register

Own the creator's portrayal choices here, separate from world facts, subject psychology and local
scene directions. Read `references/runtime/authorial-intent.md` relative to the installed `SKILL.md` for
the procedure; these authoring instructions are not links relative to a created project.
Each entry uses `### Intent <id>`, with a unique lowercase ID of letters, digits and hyphens,
starting with a letter (up to 64 characters). Its fragment is `#intent-<id>`. Repeat only for useful
scoped intentions; an unpeopled work may use this register without any persona. Empty entries are
unexamined drafts, not adopted defaults. Declared statuses are author records, not verified consent.

### Intent {intent_id}

- **status**:
  <!-- user-anchor / proposed / adopted / rejected / deferred. This entry owns its status; the ledger refers here. -->
- **basis**:
  <!-- Actual instruction, source-supported reconstruction or creator decision, including adoption scope if any. A probe is not independent corroboration. -->
- **subject_scope**:
  <!-- What this applies to: a work, subject, process, place, pattern, narrator or other target; relevant phase, continuity, viewpoint, medium or span. No required protagonist or lifelong scope. -->
- **portrayal_aim**:
  <!-- What the creator intends the audience to experience or recognize; distinguish this from what a subject wants or a world fact. Make it select among plausible realizations. -->
- **recognition_anchors**:
  <!-- The features/patterns to sustain, or a deliberate absence of a stable center. Not mandatory identical words, poses or hidden essence. -->
- **variation_envelope**:
  <!-- What may vary, what holds, and which boundary needs a new decision. A mode change can realize identity rather than violate it. -->
- **contrast_and_cadence**:
  <!-- How persistence, contrast, transitions, recurrence or omission operate over the relevant span. No compulsory switching, development, cue count or ratio. -->
- **departure_policy**:
  <!-- Treatment of meaningful revelation, disruption, lasting change, editorial redesign or indeterminacy. Identify what would need a scoped proposal, retained dimensions and aftermath; do not pre-activate future departures. -->
- **world_and_subject_dependencies**:
  <!-- Owning world/persona/identity records or explicit non-applicability. Do not duplicate facts or silently bypass physical, cultural, knowledge or adoption constraints. -->
- **priority_and_conflicts**:
  <!-- Explicit applicable precedence or unresolved conflicts; local scope alone does not authorize an override. No automatic hierarchy among themes, identity and situation. -->
- **information_boundary**:
  <!-- What is author-only, subject-known, audience-visible or intentionally withheld, for which consumer and time. Remove excluded content in a scoped view; labels are not access control. -->
- **realization_links**:
  <!-- Actual or proposed dependent rules, scenes, sequences or visual contracts. Separate present outputs from planned ones. A path/ID is not artistic evidence. -->
- **review_basis**:
  <!-- Inspected sources and span, examples, counterexamples, actual findings and remaining uncertainty. Early drafting may explicitly defer the review. -->

## Grounded state and realization interfaces

Use only where relevant. The installed `references/runtime/world-realization.md` and its local
plan coordinate references; this note must not become a duplicate event ledger or current-state
copy. Untimed work does not need an invented event order. A plan can cover a single image.

- **state_owners_and_grounding**:
  <!-- Base, event/process sources and relevant exact locators/decisions; distinguish source-explicit facts, inference, adopted design and observed output. -->
- **identity_state_and_asset_addresses**:
  <!-- Entity identity, phase/profile, state target and role-scoped reference facets. An asset hash or equal query key does not prove portrayal identity. -->
- **occurrence_presentation_and_boundary_relations**:
  <!-- Separate story targets from telling and generation order. Name actual continuity checks, ellipses, revisits, parallelism or unresolved ordering without forcing one format. -->
- **realization_and_consumer_views**:
  <!-- Which plan or scoped directions apply, what each recipient may receive, and the inspected export body. Exclude actual hidden content rather than relying on labels. -->
- **observation_repair_and_selection**:
  <!-- Link actual candidate evidence and review; distinguish state truth, source fidelity, expressive aim and perceptual quality. Retain prior candidates; bounded repairs grant no canon adoption. -->
- **dependency_change_review**:
  <!-- Changed source/intent -> potentially affected units/views -> actual reinspection. Source hashing detects edits, not semantic truth or automatic approval invalidation. -->

## Decision ledger

Use one row per cross-layer decision when decisions are being made. Local facts and behavior stay
in their owning world entry or persona ledger. An intent entry owns its portrayal rule and status;
refer to its ID here instead of maintaining a second editable decision. Status is `user-anchor`, `proposed`, `adopted`, `rejected` or `deferred`.
These prose statuses are author records, not a machine-enforced approval system.

- **candidate_records**:
  <!-- Keep incompatible options apart; hypothetical probes are not established story events. -->

For individualized portrayal, reference persona rules and EXAMPLES: held/changed conditions, context overlap, switch/recovery and an unused situation. ## Portrayal review

Use a scoped review record for a consequential realization or sequence. It is a finding, not a
second rule register. A single image or unpeopled work does not need invented dialogue or a cast.

- **authorial_intent_refs**:
  <!-- Relative Markdown links to applicable entries, e.g. [aim](#intent-i-pattern). Use n/a: reason if no intent is applicable. Also declare cross-entity dependencies in front-matter references. -->
- **review_span_and_inputs**:
  <!-- The actual scene/sequence/one-off framing, source versions or hashes, and scope not inspected. -->
- **identity_and_world_bindings**:
  <!-- Which subject profiles, facts, conditions and expressive rules apply; no psychology for an unpersonified subject. -->
- **realization_observations**:
  <!-- What actually sustains, contrasts, reveals, withholds or disrupts the intended portrayal across this span. Local plausibility is not sufficient evidence for accumulated coherence. -->
- **departure_and_aftermath**:
  <!-- Any departure decision, affected and retained dimensions, audience treatment and subsequent state or deliberate uncertainty. Otherwise record n/a with a reason. -->
- **consumer_view**:
  <!-- What was actually supplied to each downstream role; remove concealed/future information instead of relabeling it. -->
- **outcome_and_open_issues**:
  <!-- Actual observations, counterexamples, adopted/proposed revisions and remaining risks, without a quality score or manufactured approval. -->
