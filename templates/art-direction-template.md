# Art Direction Template

Use this optional working surface for the direction developed through
[Production direction](../references/runtime/production-direction.md). Record
the actual selected depiction, consequential choices and unresolved questions.
Saved production retains its existing task, specification and reference owners;
this template is not an additional approval or completeness gate. Keep only
sections that contribute to the current work.

## Image intent

Describe the result this artifact must provide and the whole depiction that
will provide it. For a scene, keep the act, state or relationship intelligible
in its circumstances. For another artifact, describe its organization and
intended experience. Include use and viewing conditions when they affect that
choice.

## Anchors

Record requirements fixed by the request or adopted sources, with their scope
and authority. These can concern identity, action, relationship, framing,
camera, medium or another property. Distinguish a supplied draft's proposed
choices from an adopted design and from the current request's fixed choices.

## Depiction requirements

Describe the meaning or information the artifact must communicate and any
relationship necessary to it. Keep those requirements separate from the pose,
view, layout or cue chosen to realize them. A detail belongs here when the
request fixes it or when it distinguishes the intended result; ordinary acts
can carry their natural construction without an inventory of body positions.

## Creative space

Record meaningful choices the author has left open and the scope in which the
agent may choose them.

## Unresolved construction question

When a question remains, state what decision would change the depicted result,
which information is missing and why it matters. Record a clarification or
consequential assumption only when one was needed. Further knowledge or local
detail serves that question.

## Selected depiction

Describe the complete selected construction and the reason for its material
choices. Its attention, subject relationships, action, arrangement and finish
should work together. Develop local shape, surface, color, lighting or detail
only to the precision the intended effect or fixed requirements need.

## Compared constructions, when relevant

Record an actual comparison when alternatives expose a consequential tradeoff
or the user requested them. Describe their different effects, what was selected
and why. A fixed or already resolved construction can proceed directly.

The Reference Use Plan, canonical Prepared Reference Set and verified Generation

source assignments and prepared artifacts through their existing procedures.

## Production-knowledge consultation

For a question actually investigated, retain the inspected source, its role and
the relationship or wording used in the selected depiction. Keep unused
possibilities distinct from adopted choices. Refer to the existing retrieval or
application record and Production Specification when those owners are present.

Available knowledge roles include an optional universal aesthetic core, an
optional concrete style family with its selected domain overlay, the realization for each
represented subject domain, a render profile or the family's base profile,
atomic modules, a scene, recipe or archetype, an activated correction and
vocabulary used for naming. Their availability does not create a slot to fill;
their distinct functions determine which source can answer the current question.

## Prompt review

Record the judgment requested by the task, using Production direction's review
process. Explain how the complete delivered input carries the intended act,
state, relationship or visual organization, and identify any supported mismatch.
An arrangement's feasibility and its effect on the requested result are separate
judgments. Let that comparison select the local question needing investigation.

Keep the evidence for fixed requirements and necessary relationships at their
actual scopes. Subject-domain construction, gaze, support, contact, coverage,
rendering grammar, surface, color, light and detail hierarchy contribute when
they affect that judgment. An ordinary complete concept can carry a relationship
without another clause specifying its individual positions.

When references are involved, use the current authoritative count, order, source
and transport hashes. Assess identity and target-scene requirements against their
assigned sources: a scene-only role cannot establish permanent identity, and an
identity-only source does not supply the new action, state or rendering context.
Keep incidental source narrative, planning commentary and the review itself
outside the model-facing depiction.

## Negative-source provenance

Record the exclusions actually selected, their scopes and their sources under
the active negative policy. Sources can include generation hygiene, applicable
visible-structure bundles, the selected render-profile boundary, an activated
correction or an explicit user exclusion. Diagnostic knowledge that informed
review retains that role instead of becoming an automatic prompt exclusion.
