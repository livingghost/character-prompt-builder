# Anthro Prompt Anatomy

## Identity block

Define the species anatomy before clothing and scene:

```text
anthropomorphic black wolf; upright triangular ears; long structured canine muzzle;
large paw-like hands with dark claws; powerful canine lower legs; one large coherent tail
```

For hybrids, assign regions explicitly:

```text
wolf head and tail, broad feline torso markings, digitigrade canine legs
```

## Body and proportion block

Avoid a single vague word such as `muscular`. Use the visible structure that matters:

```text
massive broad shoulders, thick trapezius, deep chest, narrow waist,
large forearms, powerful thighs, stable head-to-body ratio
```

When the desired design is cute, childlike, chibi, mature, elderly, or otherwise age-coded, state it as a normal proportion and facial-design choice. Do not add such a descriptor when it was not requested.

## Surface block

### Short fur

```text
short close-lying matte fur grouped into broad color planes,
sparse tufts at the cheeks, neck ruff, elbows, calves, and outer silhouette
```

### Long fur

Define mass and direction first:

```text
layered long fur forming a heavy neck mantle and forearm tufts,
large coherent clumps following gravity and body movement
```

### Scales, feathers, and plates

Use species-appropriate terms instead of automatically adding fur:

```text
small matte scales with larger throat plates
layered contour feathers with broad wing-cover shapes
segmented chitin plates with restrained highlights
```

## Coat and marking block

Use location and symmetry:

```text
charcoal base coat; lighter muzzle, throat, chest, and inner forearms;
three faded diagonal scars on the character's right upper arm;
identical amber eye design in every image
```

## Wardrobe block

Describe garment construction and material separation:

```text
matte black leather vest with a continuous front opening, silver studs,
one chest chain attached between two rings, fitted dark woven shirt,
dark cargo pants with one thigh pocket, heavy lace-up combat boots
```

For near-black outfits, separate materials by value and highlight behavior so fur, leather, cotton, and metal do not merge.

## Action and contact block

State paths and endpoints:

```text
The left forearm rises in front of the torso and the left hand pinches the chain's top link.
The chain hangs freely in one continuous curve without wrapping around the tail.
The right hand rests beside the belt buckle.
```

## Camera block

Use crop, angle, and perspective strength:

```text
full-body three-quarter view, eye level slightly below the chest,
moderate low-angle emphasis, controlled perspective, both boots fully visible
```

## Rendering block

Describe measurable visual behavior:

```text
crisp tapered linework, bold outer contour, thinner interior anatomy lines,
flat matte local colors, two-step hard-edged cel shading,
controlled soft highlights, simplified fur planes, minimal background texture
```

## Constraint block

Keep constraints concrete and relevant:

```text
exactly two arms and two hands; exactly one tail; one continuous chain;
complete boots and soles inside frame; readable garment seams;
no accidental text, watermark, or signature
```

## Frame character block

State the skeletal and surface character before muscular detail when it affects identity. Keep body build and frame character separate.

Example diagnostic pattern for a thick-boned smooth or rounded subject:

```text
Under the outer surface, the frame is thick-boned and the visible masses stay smooth and rounded. The cheek forms one full convex volume from the eye line to the jaw, the cheekbone landmark remains submerged, the broad jaw turns through a soft rounded corner, and the joints and muscle handoffs use the same rounded structural language. Muscularity does not introduce chiseled facial planes or hollow cheeks.
```

This is a candidate structural pattern, not a universal wording requirement. Adapt it to the declared species, surface system, and style family.

## Measurement and accessory block

Before prose assembly, list any load-bearing relative measures and accessory geometry. Examples include muzzle length against head depth, earring diameter against ear height, tag width against nose width, chain unit against tag width, and buckle span against palm width. Preserve count, side, support, layer order, and pose response in the affirmative construction text.
