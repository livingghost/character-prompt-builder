# Character Performance Review Probes

Use with [Character Performance](character-performance.md). These hypothetical, scoped method
examples show what a reviewer must examine; nothing in them is a persona to copy, a biological
claim or an adopted event, and text-hygiene or schema tests establish none of the results below.

## 1. One person: emotion, addressee and audience do not share one switch

Example proposal: an agent values checking an item before judging its owner, and notices missing
steps in an explanation. In this invented setting, public service is spoken in formal polite forms;
this individual also uses clipped plain forms with one trusted colleague in private. The declared
expressive channels are speech, gaze and manual manipulation of a document. These are proposed
rules rather than evidence from a work, and a demographic label establishes none of them.

The hypothetical persona owns `voice-public-check`, `voice-private-check` and
`response-interrupted-check`. Public politeness is held under irritation, while repeated
unsupported certainty removes hedges and shortens the explanation. Private access changes the
forms; the requirement to check stays. A bystander matters to the mode even when the words go to
someone else. The body can continue holding a document after the speaking pace recovers. These
rules bind only this person in this world, whatever language another world uses.

| Controlled condition | Hypothetical realization | What is being examined |
|---|---|---|
| Routine public question | A complete, formal reply that names the record and gives the reason before the answer. The document is placed where both can see it. | Question target and reason-before-answer sequence, not a generic courteous adjective |
| Same public question; an unsupported conclusion is repeated | The same formal endings, a shorter explanation and a request for the original record. The agent keeps the document in place and stops offering additional explanations. | Polite endings held; hedges and explanation length change; no automatic shout or scowl |
| The trusted colleague asks the same thing, but a public listener remains | The agent retains the public forms and points to the disputed entry. | Addressee alone does not activate this proposed private mode |
| The public listener leaves | After the door closes: a clipped, informal remark that the figures still disagree and a direct request to look. The pace relaxes before the document is released. | Explicit switch point; linguistic change and bodily recovery are not simultaneous |
| Public context remains, but the voice is temporarily strained | The same check/request sequence is conveyed more quietly or through an established written channel. | A resource constraint changes delivery, not necessarily courtesy, motive or certainty |

These are samples; a review can find them too alike, too schematic or unsupported by the actual
adopted persona. Record that result and revise only the owning rules within the authorized scope.
A fresh probe, unused when the initial rule was written, might concern an unexpected gift during
unfinished work: determine whether the rule is sufficient, irrelevant or in conflict with another
adopted rule. An invented answer stays an invention, however it is labeled.

## 2. Emotion is not fully legible, and unchanged speech can be correct

A different proposal states that a person has the same short, matter-of-fact delivery when
content and when afraid, because attention remains on an immediate task. Another observer, hearing
that delivery, is left guessing at the feeling. The author may know more than the viewpoint does.

Review whether the scene respects that limit. Three additions would break it:

- a trembling hand added as a compulsory clue;
- the hidden feeling announced in limited narration;
- every sentence ending changed merely to prove variability.

A condition that leaves the voice unchanged by design is a useful control case. A later outburst
or private disclosure is optional; suppression can simply continue.

## 3. A non-vocal subject or a functional system

A declared subject communicates in an authored signed language. Its linguistic register and
turn-taking belong in SPEECH, which stays filled although vocal timbre is absent, and the entry
describes linguistic forms rather than a decorative hand-wave. Limit descriptive claims to what
the supplied world and language record supports; an undecided linguistic realization stays a gap
rather than an invented account of a real signed language.

Separately, an unpersonified controller has a status indicator and pause/retry logic. A delayed
signal is an operation; anxiety or a suppressed human motive would be an invention, and a persona
may be absent entirely. Confirm that the selected medium and any visual record preserve the
declared operation, with face, voice, hidden emotion and protagonist all left uninvented.

## 4. Single image versus a changing response

A prose beat may depict a glance stopping, a reply continuing, a grip tightening and then
loosening. A still image must select one of those moments. Review whether its visible cue geometry
is possible at that instant and whether the prompt mistakenly asks for both the grip and its
release at once. Unseen words and hidden motives belong in author direction; a claim about pixels
covers only what the pixels show. For audio, a visible glance is inaudible, so the beat needs an
audible carrier unless that omission is intentional.

## 5. Similarity, scope and revision

Two people may share an address form, short apology or whole workplace routine. A literal
duplicate finding only prompts inspection, and keeping that overlap may be the correct decision.
Meaningful contrast, where requested, can lie in attention, choice, listening or physical timing
rather than an accent or a unique pronoun. Cast size is the work's choice, and a pairwise contrast
grid is optional.

A proposed voice revision leaves the world convention, relationship status, language competence
and visual anatomy as they are; a change to any of them is named and adopted on its own. A future
adopted change gets its proper time scope, and an exercise that invents a situation records an
exercise rather than an event. Review the resulting portrayal and list unresolved limits;
template density and these examples establish nothing about quality.
