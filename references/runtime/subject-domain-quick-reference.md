# Subject-domain quick reference

Load this compact reference only when a prompt-only request needs domain routing. The lists below are optional domain examples, not required structures or absence checklists. Use the user anchors and the adopted declaration; do not silently normalize custom, missing or asymmetric structures to a typical specimen.

## Human

Record stable facial structure, skin and hair traits, body build, age or presentation, hands, nails, scars, tattoos, and clothing geometry. Separate stable identity from temporary expression, sweat, injury, lighting, or pose.

## Ordinary animal

Keep natural anatomy, locomotion, limb count, paw or hoof structure, muzzle, ears, tail, coat or scale pattern, and realistic articulation distinct from anthropomorphic anatomy. Do not silently add human shoulders, hands, clothing posture, or bipedal balance.

## Anthropomorphic animal

Declare the animal taxon and the degree of humanlike structure. Resolve muzzle length and width, nose construction, ear shape, digitigrade or plantigrade stance, hand or paw terminals, tail origin and length, coat distribution, markings, and expressive channels such as ears, tail, whiskers, crest, or hackles. Feline and canine identification must follow the explicit species anchor rather than role or color similarity.

## Robot or mechanical character

Record chassis silhouette, segment count, joint type, terminal effectors, sensor layout, display surfaces, lighting channels, materials, and permitted deformation. Expression may use pose, optic shape, display graphics, light intensity, sound, vents, or appendage orientation rather than human facial assumptions.

## Skeletal, undead, or nonstandard body plan

Declare which anatomical structures exist, their count, attachment points, articulation, surface covering, and expression channels. Do not infer missing soft-tissue landmarks. Keep impossible or fantastical anatomy explicit rather than normalizing it toward a human template.

## Invented organism or hybrid

For recurring identity or reconstruction-critical structure, create a morphology contract using [Declared Structures Runtime](declared-structures.md). Declare only the actual structures, connections, surfaces and variations that matter. A short exploratory draft need not create a complete state graph. Use semantic preflight to check declared counts and ownership without typical-anatomy defaults.
