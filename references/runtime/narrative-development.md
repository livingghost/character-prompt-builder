# World-Coherent Creative Development

Apply [Production direction](production-direction.md) inside the existing
[production run](production-execution.md), in this order:

1. the full applicable design and persona;
2. purpose-specific choices;
3. the selected realization;
4. the actual evidence;
5. scoped repair.

[Permissions](production-permissions.md) separate delegated judgment from submission, selection
and canonical adoption. The run keeps its single evaluation ledger (review record); cast, emotion
curve and stock gesture stay optional.

## Activation and ownership

Use this route to develop or revise any of these layers, and whenever a production exposes a
conflict between them:

- world, premise, theme or genre treatment;
- character, relationship or situation;
- narrative or dialogue.

Deliver the artifact asked for. Thinking needs none of file creation, packs, a Studio or a model.
Ordinary isolated image wording follows its own runtime.

The creative unit is the work in its intended scope. It may have zero, one or many personified
subjects, and a fixed, changing or distributed focus does not make the work an ensemble drama. A
location, process, institution, object, atmosphere or formal pattern can organize the work while
staying impersonal. Genre, theme, romance, conflict, growth arc, dramatic climax and moral
conclusion are all optional.

This document owns the creative method. The other owners:

- [Narrative Authoring](../narrative-authoring.md) owns local files and commands.
- [Narrative Protocol](../narrative-protocol.md) owns shared JSON fields and approval.
- The [Project Design Template](../../templates/narrative/design/design-template.md) is a scope
  and dependency workspace rather than a second story schema.
- The complete [Persona Template](../../templates/narrative/personas/persona-template.md) owns
  detailed agent portrayal while it is designed or materially revised.

Read the existing world and design files together with any persona.

## 1. Establish the work's current design frame

Start from the user's actual entry point; a world-first, event-first, theme-first,
character-first or image-first idea keeps that order. Recover existing anchors and adoption
records before adding anything. Delegated invention permits proposals; approval stays with the
user. An answered question stays answered, and a rejected suggestion stays rejected.

Record what serves this task in a project design note, in conversation or in `narrative/design/`.
Read the full design template, then work on the relevant sections. Each form is a working record,
not a questionnaire to send wholesale to the user.

| Design dimension | Decision to expose rather than assume |
|---|---|
| Output and scope | What is being made now, its medium, extent, intended use and completion boundary |
| Anchors and freedoms | User requirements, adopted decisions, provisional choices, exclusions and open questions |
| Experience and inquiry | What the work invites the audience to notice, feel or question; themes may be plural, implicit, emergent or intentionally unresolved |
| World and possibility | What exists, how it operates, where and when rules apply, and what remains unexplained |
| Focus and access | What receives attention, who or what acts, and whose knowledge or senses constrain the telling |
| Form and expression | Genre conventions actually chosen, tone, diction, rhythm, visual grammar and intentional transitions |
| Continuity and change | What must hold, what can vary, over which times, places, viewpoints, phases or branches |
| Decision ownership | Where each fact or choice is authoritative, its status, dependencies and affected outputs |

Theme is not a compulsory message, and participants may disagree with it. Genre chooses
conventions only, and tone can shift. A work may mix genres, pursue competing themes, change
emphasis or refuse closure; record the scope and presentation of those choices instead of
flattening them into one mood.

Coherence means that the work's elements can coexist under its adopted rules, distinctions and
presentation choices. It does not require realism, agreement among characters, identical local
cultures, a fully explained cosmology, a single timeline or exhaustive worldbuilding. An accepted
mystery can stay unknown; an unexamined dependency is a gap rather than intentional ambiguity.

### Retain the creator's portrayal aims

For a recurring identity, expressive pattern or consequential local framing, read
[Authorial Intent](authorial-intent.md) and record in the design register:

- the actual aim, its scope and its status;
- recognition anchors and the variation envelope (the permitted range);
- cadence and departure policy.

The aim may concern an unpeopled place or a process as well as a person. A stable center,
designed switching and deliberate indeterminacy are all possible designs.

Use the aim to select between plausible realizations, then review its accumulated effect over the
requested span. A lifelong essence, a required dramatic contrast and drift excused as variety all
lie outside the aim. A purposeful break needs its own scoped basis and treatment; ordinary
variation stays ordinary.

## Ground state and prepare only the requested realization

For consequential continuity or recurring portrayals, activate
[World Realization](world-realization.md). Before writing or rendering the requested unit, bind:

- the source or decision basis;
- canonical state;
- current information access;
- authorial intent.

Reuse the existing event resolver as the only current-world ledger. Story order, presentation
order and generation order differ; assert continuity only where the work requires it.

The local realization plan can:

- pin world, persona and intent sources;
- resolve independent boundaries;
- prepare role-specific reference queries;
- export an explicit recipient view.

Untimed or unresolved chronology can stay in the design rather than in that serializer. Output is
reviewed separately against source, state and creator intent. Bounded repairs preserve prior
candidates, and an observed accident enters canon only through adoption.

[Portrayal Principles](../portrayal-principles.md) is an optional CPB-authored pattern dictionary
and supplies patterns only: read complete selected records and instantiate them under scoped
design decisions.

## 2. Develop the relevant dependencies, in either direction

Identify what the requested output depends on and work on the most consequential uncertainty
first. The loop:

1. Inspect the existing design.
2. Propose locally.
3. Trace implications.
4. Test the relevant interfaces.
5. Retain or revise.

Dependencies run both ways: world rules can shape a person's opportunities, a convincing person can expose a missing institution, and an event can reveal a rule that needs an explicit exception. Every layer stays open while the others are incomplete. Build
enough world for the present work and its likely continuations. State which dependencies were
checked, deferred or judged inapplicable. Let detail density follow causal and expressive
importance rather than headcount.

### Claims, mechanisms and what sustains them

A compressed description is an entry point rather than a finished design: a long trust, a weekly
visit, a private ritual, a reputation, a refusal, an attachment to a place. Inspect what the claim
asserts, which conditions let it exist, what began it, what repeats it and what keeps it possible
now. These are exploration tools, never a questionnaire returned to the user.

Do not answer an open basis with a synonym; "they cooperate because they trust each other" has not
said how trust operates. Propose mechanisms that differ in kind before choosing a draft direction:
repeated ordinary contact, a shared practice, an institutional assignment, a material constraint,
a valued difference, a changed opportunity, one incident, or an accumulation with no identifiable
onset. Options that differ only in intensity or wording are not alternatives. Keep the comparison
and the chosen reason as authored notes.

For a consequential recurring condition, these roles are distinct; they may share an event or have
none:

| Role | Question |
|---|---|
| Opportunity | What makes contact or action possible here? |
| Formation | What experience, process or predisposition contributes? |
| First instance | What starts this practice, where an identifiable start matters? |
| Repetition | Why does it happen again? |
| Current maintenance | Which present conditions and choices sustain it now? |
| Recognition | When does the person reinterpret what already exists? |
| Presentation | What does the audience learn, and when? |

A reaction is not its own origin, a display of loyalty does not establish how loyalty formed, and
one experience does not produce a whole personality. Before asserting a cause, instantiate it:
who was present, what was possible, what was known, what happened or recurred, and what endured.
Omit an irrelevant question rather than invent content for it.

Each step backward returns sideways: the same period's work, other relationships, travel, hours,
tools, money, places, institutions and seasons, to the extent the work needs. Give a supporting
person a present purpose of their own and a consequential place usable conditions; a background
process stays impersonal, and not every thread returns as a clue or a lesson. Before adding a
person, follow [Cast Admission and Persona Depth](cast-and-persona-depth.md). Then move forward
from the proposed circumstances and ask what they would produce without assuming the wanted
payoff. A stable center is optional, and an outward reversal proves no unchanged essence.

Test the design in an ordinary situation with an immediate task, real objects, a time and the
information available, and then at a boundary: a colleague trusted while their proposal is
refused, a routine outliving its first practical reason. A sample tests a proposal; it is neither a
prior event nor evidence for its own rule.

### World, society and material conditions

For a consequential rule, identify its subject, scope, trigger, operation, effects, persistence
and any actual limits or exceptions. Costs are authored choices, and a system may have none. A
rule may be ordinary, fantastic, symbolic, probabilistic, unreliable in-world or only partly
disclosed. Distinguish objective conditions from a group's explanation, a character's belief, a
narrator's assertion and an author's unshown plan.

Trace effects across relevant scales: access to a resource or ability can affect travel, work,
communication, institutions, routines, objects and language. A spectacular ability revises the
consequences that presupposed its absence. Propose the material consequences with their basis and
scope; the rest stay proposals at most. An institution can hold many wills; record diversity,
roles and local practice where it matters.

### Agents and persona

When individualized behavior, dialogue or continuing identity matters, read the complete persona
form and the existing applicable persona files first. Keep all twenty sections as the workspace:
deepen causally relevant fields first and retain the rest as examined states or visible gaps. A
summary may support comparison; the detailed form stays the record. Personas belong to portrayed
agents; scenery, forces, decorative crowds and collectives get one only when portrayed as agents,
whatever a file format expects, and an impersonal process gets a process model.

For each relevant agent, connect wants, values, competence, knowledge, habits, speech and choices
to their circumstances while keeping the person larger than them. Distinguish self-image,
observable behavior and outside perception, and an immediate feeling from the action that
follows. Category labels, physique, reputation and social role leave temperament open. An agent
needs an existence beyond a plot function or a contrast with another agent. Who joins the cast, on
whose decision, and how much of a person each use needs:
[Cast Admission and Persona Depth](cast-and-persona-depth.md).

For individualized voice and embodied expression, activate
[Character Performance](character-performance.md). It binds persona section 2 to the governing
authorial intent, sets scoped baselines and ranges, resolves each condition under its own rule,
and tests the actual language and bodily changes; the test, rather than the filled form,
demonstrates the portrayal.

Use THOUGHT, SPEECH, PERSONALITY and EXAMPLES to test conditional decisions and exact language;
use SOCIAL and RELATIONSHIPS only for interactions relevant to this portrayal. Record directional
ties independently rather than forcing mutuality or completing every pair in a cast. A
personality can be complete while lacking conflict, intimacy, trauma or personal growth.

Original proposals and hypothetical design samples are authored tests; record their design basis,
and keep sources and reconstruction separate for existing subjects. A form is filled from real
observations, quotations and corroboration or left with a visible gap; depth targets are met with
examined content rather than filler.

### Themes, events and form

Explore the work's concerns through the chosen material rather than by making every event prove
a thesis. An event may simply sustain attention or maintain a routine.
A scene need not change a relationship, contain dialogue, advance an individual arc or end
differently from how it began.

Choose causal, observational, associative, cyclic, episodic, branching, documentary or other
organization as the work needs. Distinguish story order, presentation order and audience
knowledge. A genre transition or deliberate contradiction gets an authored scope and treatment;
consistency means recording it, and an accidental contradiction is recorded as accidental.

## 3. Make alternatives along the requested axis

Before generating alternatives, state what remains fixed and what may vary. Each of these is a
different comparison task:

- personality designs;
- different events with the same people;
- world mechanisms, themes or viewpoints;
- plots or endings;
- renditions of one scene.

Preserve adopted decisions outside that axis. A situation variation does not silently redesign its
cast, a character variation leaves the world's laws in place, and a genre experiment leaves events
and ethics as adopted. When an option requires a cross-layer change, name the change and its
consequences in the proposal. Keep incompatible branches separate instead of averaging them or
leaking facts between them.

When alternatives are requested, offer a manageable set of substantive ones, each with why it fits
the current work, its important tradeoff and its open dependency. The count of people and
candidate situations stays open, and a local refinement may produce a single refinement.

## 4. Test coherence at the interfaces

For a proposed passage, scene, world entry, character or visual treatment, ask what licenses each
consequential statement and choice. A compact working trace is enough:

`current frame and state -> relevant rules and viewpoints -> depicted action or observation ->
what changes or deliberately persists -> consequences and dependencies for later work`

An arrow can be a physical cause or an associative or formal relation; name the relation when
that is the chosen method. Trace an agent's decisive behavior to the active persona, situation,
knowledge or a deliberately authored change, and an unpeopled scene to environment, process and
presentation. An author's desired ending still needs a world or an agent able to produce it.

Review these interfaces to the depth the output needs:

| Interface | Useful test |
|---|---|
| World to world | Do rules, locations, institutions and material conditions agree within their declared scope? Are exceptions bounded rather than convenient rescues? |
| World to agent | Can the agent know, learn, obtain and do this here and now? Does behavior remain individualized rather than stereotyped? |
| Agent to agent | Are each participant's choices and interpretations supported? Is a directional change actually earned, when one is intended? |
| Event to continuity | Are time, access, possessions, knowledge and lasting consequences compatible with preceding and following material? |
| Work to theme | Does the material engage, complicate or deliberately set aside the declared concern without compulsory moral agreement? |
| Telling to information | Is the viewpoint allowed this information? Are concealment, revelation and ambiguity handled as designed? |
| Content to expression | Do language, imagery, rhythm and genre shifts support the adopted experience without becoming a uniform style mandate? |
| Local revision to whole | Which facts, scenes, personas, visual contracts and approvals depend on the changed decision? Which remain valid? |

Use counterexamples as well as a favorable sample: a rule at its boundary, a persona in a
different but relevant context, a revision against a later dependent scene. Before repairing a
problem, classify it:

- an actual contradiction;
- an unsupported inference;
- a purposeful contrast;
- a scoped exception;
- an unresolved dependency;
- an intentional unknown.

Look also for these failures:

- a label used as its own explanation;
- a display or a recognition mistaken for formation;
- a new event that silently rewrites an accepted person;
- every independent thread converging on one wanted conclusion;
- present knowledge leaking backward in time;
- a supporting person with no purpose except steering the main cast;
- a new proposal reported as an old decision.

Coherence leaves most things unconnected and most minor choices unexplained; work on the
interfaces that matter. The review stays a scoped inspection rather than a universal score or a
genre preference.

## 5. Adopt locally and propagate changes deliberately

Keep user anchors and proposed, adopted, rejected and deferred decisions distinct. Record the
scope, basis, controlling file or section and actual adoption where it exists. Approval is a
recorded decision per item: selecting an event approves that event alone, and incidental names, a
whole persona, an ending or a new world law each need their own adoption. A saved file or a
syntactically valid JSON document is only a file.

Each kind of record has one home:

- cross-layer rationale and impact records in `narrative/design/`;
- entity facts in their owning world files;
- behavior in the persona;
- structured narrative intent in `narrative.json`;
- executed state in state artifacts.

Link and summarize rather than create competing canonical copies. The persona's Design Ledger
owns local behavior decisions; the project ledger references them when they affect other layers,
and a standalone persona keeps that scope in its own ledger.

A world rule or theme can change across an authored phase, region, branch or revision. Record
what changed, where it applies, why, what remains valid and which dependent artifacts need
review; a retcon stays visible. A fictional contradiction between perspectives may be
intentional; inconsistent editorial copies of an adopted fact are a mistake to repair.

Before delivery, report the artifact's actual status and material gaps. A focused draft can be
ready for comparison while the rest of the world stays open, and a small requested output
proceeds with its unknowns listed.

## 6. Connect to the existing narrative and production contracts

Initialize files only when persistent authoring helps. The neutral seed starts with empty cast,
themes, arcs and chapters; the design note records whether each empty dimension is undecided or
intentionally unused. The explicit example seed is a teaching scaffold; protagonist count and
structure come from the work. Choose the medium for the task; the CLI's default `screen` is only a
default.

| Design responsibility | Owning artifact or field |
|---|---|
| Scope, genre treatment, theme exploration, formal rules, decision provenance and impact | Local `narrative/design/*.md` notes; these are authoring records, not shared JSON or execution approvals |
| Stable places, institutions, mechanisms, groups and objects | `world/locations`, `factions`, `systems`, `artifacts`; shared terms in `glossary` |
| Declared thematic concerns and organizing threads, when used | `narrative.themes`, typed `arcs`, chapter `depicts`; no fixed theme count or forced thread |
| Individually portrayed agents and their active phases | Character `persona`, `phases`, `from_chapter`, `changed`, `held` |
| Relevant directional ties, when used | `relationships.from`, `to`, `bond_type`, `at_start` and associated arcs |
| Intended setups, questions and actually established learning | `promises`, `questions`, `knowledge`; planned facts are not learned facts |
| The authored scene and its treatment | `scene-plot` proposition, beats, focalization, setting, optional turn and structure, realization |
| Interaction-specific change, only when it occurs | `relationship_delta` with a supported channel and a visible beat `from` |
| Persistent consequences, only when they occur | `state_changes`, then separately approved state events if using state runtime |

These are destinations; the protocol's exact fields still need reading. Empty cast and thread
lists, zero exchanges, an absent relationship delta and an omitted turn are all supported. A
scene still declares what is presented, where and how; a held condition is recorded as held. The
`focal_beat` unit key identifies its focal visible beat even when it depicts persistence. Named
structures and arc developmental sections are optional; when used, the latter describe the actual
organization rather than mandatory escalation.

The JSON protocol currently serializes ordered chapters and scenes in prose, comics, screen or
mixed realization. It is not a native interactive branch graph or a format for every possible
medium. Keep unsupported structures in design documents, or create explicitly scoped serialized
paths when useful, and report the limitation; invented fields and a work reshaped to pass
validation are both falsifications.

For each scene involving portrayed agents, select the phase in force for that chapter and read
the relevant personas in full with the applicable world records and current state. A persona
applies from its first applicable phase onward. A limited-viewpoint consumer (a downstream
writer, actor or renderer) receives only what that viewpoint can know. The author's knowledge,
another viewpoint and a later phase stay out of its input by removal; an author-only label alone
leaves them in.

A durable portrayal change gets a new persona file with explicit `changed` and `held`; a passing
mood or temporary injury stays state. Phases start at chapter boundaries in the present contract:
depict an intra-chapter change in its actual beat and interim state, then open the new phase at
the next boundary. An editorial correction is an edit rather than a life phase.

JSON approval hashes cover the JSON documents, not the bytes of linked Markdown, and persona
fingerprints are informational. Record reviewed versions of relevant design, world and persona
files in the handoff, compare them after edits and re-review affected outputs. A Markdown edit
currently leaves every JSON approval standing; report that limit as it is.

For visuals, project what is actually visible and appropriate to the chosen expression; hidden
world facts and the persona dossier stay out of the prompt. Narrative PHYSICAL notes summarize
embodiment; adopted visual identity and declared structures take precedence over them. Use
[Character Sheet Discipline](character-sheet-discipline.md) for identity, the relevant prompt
runtime and its retrieval gates for wording, and [State-Aware Series Runtime](state-aware-series.md)
for stateful images. Requesting generation and promoting a candidate are separate explicit steps
that narrative work by itself stops short of.

For worked boundary cases, use [Coherence Review Probes](narrative-coherence-probes.md), which
are examples of scoped review rather than required genres or shapes.

## Checks and limits

Use the index and coverage reports in Narrative Authoring. They check declared links, drafting
syntax, timing, phase selection and supported artifact contracts, and a design root can connect
world files before any scene exists. They do not infer semantic dependencies from prose, prove
that a rule is believable, determine a theme's meaning or establish adoption. Neither can establish
world coherence or artistic adequacy; the interface review above belongs to the agent or author,
who states the inspected scope and unresolved risks honestly.

Use `--strict` for a declared no-gap checkpoint; exploratory drafts stay allowed, and a world fact
unused by any scene is a coverage gap rather than a contradiction. Tests are structural regression
checks and worked authoring probes; the judgment of fiction stays with people.

Regression entrypoints:

- `python scripts/world_coherence_smoke_test.py`
- `python scripts/persona_workflow_smoke_test.py`
- `python scripts/narrative_authoring_smoke_test.py`

They run locally and skip image generation. They exercise neutral initialization, world-to-design
links, zero or arbitrary cast size, non-dramatic scenes, variant boundaries, full-form persona
handling and narrative joins.


## Artifact evidence and completion

For a saved deliverable, continue through [Production Execution](production-execution.md),
keeping this document's own interpretation, retrieval, approval and adoption boundaries:

1. Prepare the exact inputs.
2. Capture the real output.
3. Bind review and selection to it.
4. Complete and close the work task.

`scripts/production_workflow.py status`, `impact` and `resume` recheck dependencies and artifact
bytes. Completion and canonical adoption are explicit recorded steps; a progress checkbox, a
search hit or a newly created image is none of them.

## Prepared scene Persona material

During scene preparation, read the complete applicable Persona definitions, their portrayal
model, authorial intent and current conditions. Save the relevant definition text,
cross-definition dependencies, partner-specific exceptions and scoped applications with
[Scene Persona Material](scene-persona.md) as a record of understanding after reading; that
document also states when a reviewed bundle's `persona.md` is reused for execution, local repair
and resumption, and which changed conditions reopen the preparation. The material is authoring
input rather than canon or a performer's knowledge. Required text is saved in full; a size measure
says nothing about semantic completeness, and full reading and its scope review remain attributed
judgments.
