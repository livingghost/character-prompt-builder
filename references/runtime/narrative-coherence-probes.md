# Coherence review probes

Use these worked counterexamples to exercise the method rather than as a genre list, required
plots or model-generated quality evidence. Each changes what matters to the work while keeping the
same review responsibilities. The CLI regressions check data and links; the reasoning below is an
author or agent review rather than a claim that software understands fictional meaning.

## A world atlas before any story

The requested artifact is a map and short account of a region where light repeats at different
intervals in different valleys; agents, theme, plot and conflict are all undeclared.

Begin with geography and the scope of the light rule, tracing only the consequences for mapping,
travel conditions and the meaning of local time terms. Record the mechanism as a system, the
valleys as locations and the shared vocabulary as terms, connected through a design note. Leave
the origin unexplained if that is the design, and leave inhabitants, a protagonist and a plot
uninvented; the design note links the files.

Counterexample: one map labels all valleys with a single universal dawn while the adopted rule
makes their illumination asynchronous. Ask whether the labels are an explicit external time
standard or an accidental contradiction. Fix the time representation or revise the rule with its
dependencies, rather than adding a character who explains away the mismatch.

## An observation without agents or a turn

The requested work is a short prose observation of still water under sustained light. Its function
is attention and rhythm rather than revelation or transformation; the scene has a setting and
visible material and lacks cast, speech, relationship delta, explicit theme and persistent
consequence.

Keep world conditions, spatial placement, viewpoint and cadence compatible. A supported scene uses
empty reference and state-change lists and omits `turn`, or explicitly records persistence; a
personified landscape is optional. An author can later decide that a theme is useful; it then
enters the record as a later decision rather than an initial user instruction.

Counterexample: an added dramatic gust contradicts the adopted stillness, and change by itself
falls short of improvement. Preserve the requested condition unless the variation axis explicitly
includes it.

## A solo investigation in a speculative setting

A lone investigator reconstructs a remote failure. The world's communication delay is fixed; the
investigator's persona distinguishes cautious inference from confident public speech. The
project's concern with trust is an inquiry rather than a requirement that the investigator learn
a lesson.

A clue is usable only if it can reach the investigator through the system at the relevant time.
The active persona shapes what they infer and say; whether the world permits instant access is the
system's rule. A scene can advance knowledge while personality stays unchanged and the lead stays
single.

Counterexample: a proposed solution relies on a future transmission or knowledge visible only to
the author. Change the information route, timing or proposal, or explicitly revise the system and
review every dependent scene; inaccessible information stays inaccessible however good the
dramatic line.

## A routine, with no obligatory growth

A comedic sequence observes a familiar maintenance routine. Its agents retain their preferences;
small exchanges create rhythm rather than intimacy, conflict or a character arc. The chosen
institution and equipment make the routine possible.

Use persona to make behavior and exact language reproducible, and the world records to check
access, roles and practical affordances. Do not manufacture a secret wound, a corrective moral or
a reversal for a scene whose intended effect is recognition and repetition.

Counterexample: an improvised repair ignores an adopted operating condition. A comic exaggeration
can be intentional, given the work's actual presentation license; otherwise repair the technical
assumption and leave the character's whole personality alone.

## Changing focus without requiring an ensemble drama

A work follows one object through unrelated locations and times; many agents may appear, or none
in some sections. The organizing subject is circulation and material change rather than a network
of relationships between all participants.

Track the object's properties and time-scoped possession, access, relevant local cultures and
information available in each section. Build full personas only where individualized behavior is
authored; off-screen lives stay out of the delivered scene, ties between every pair stay
uninvented, and the object's movement may stay free of a human desire.

Counterexample: a later section shows an already-spent component intact, with restoration,
branching or another declared mechanism absent. Review artifact state and chronology; a large
cast or a changing focal participant is by itself insufficient reason to label the whole work an
ensemble drama.

## Competing accounts, changing genres and plural themes

A work first presents an unexplained event with a comic register, later revisits it with dread,
and leaves the accounts of its witnesses in disagreement. The design may hold multiple themes and
refuse to settle one ultimate explanation.

Separate stable occurrences, contested beliefs, what the narrator asserts, and the audience's
access to each. Genre and tone can change through framing and information rather than retroactive
changes to established world facts. Apparent coherence is insufficient reason to force all
witnesses into one moral position or to make the story uniformly ominous.

Counterexample: an editorial note holds both a rule and its negation as unqualified adopted facts
in the same scope. That becomes an unreliable-narrator technique only once a real viewpoint,
exception, phase or unresolved status is established; otherwise revise the contradictory copies.
Preserve intentional ambiguity while keeping accidental editorial drift visible as drift.

## Alternatives and unsupported form

A request for three treatments of an adopted event varies viewpoint and rhythm while the cast's
personality and the world's laws stay locked; state that baseline before making the treatments. A
proposal that changes a rule belongs to a separately labeled design branch with an impact review.

A branching interactive work may stay in design records, or have explicitly scoped paths projected
to supported chapter sequences; the current scene serializer handles chapter sequences rather than
a native branch graph. Do not report a note as an executable branch, merge contradictory outcomes
into one timeline, or rename the work as screen fiction to satisfy the serializer.

These examples exercise scope, rules, agents, themes, events, information, expression and revision
impact in different combinations. None is a mandatory structure for a new brief.
