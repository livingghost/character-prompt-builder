# Identity-Led Contextual Voice and Embodied Performance

Apply [Production direction](production-direction.md) inside the existing
[production run](production-execution.md), in this order:

1. the full applicable design and persona;
2. purpose-specific choices;
3. the selected realization;
4. the actual evidence;
5. scoped repair.

[Permissions](production-permissions.md) separate delegated judgment from submission, selection
and canonical adoption. The run keeps its single evaluation ledger (review record); cast, emotion
curve and stock gesture stay optional.

## Activation and scope

Read this route when designing, diagnosing or materially revising individualized behavior,
dialogue, vocal delivery, expression, movement or their changes, and when a detailed persona
exists but the actual portrayal is interchangeable, context-blind or loses its intended center.
For initial preparation, or for work outside reviewed scene material, read:

- the complete [Persona Template](../../templates/narrative/personas/persona-template.md);
- the applicable persona phase;
- its governing authorial intents;
- the relevant world and current-state records.

An adopted persona stays as adopted, however much color one scene would gain from a redesign.

[World-Coherent Creative Development](narrative-development.md) owns the work's method and scope.
[Authorial Intent](authorial-intent.md) owns the creator's portrayal aims, scope, constancy and
contrast, deliberate departures and cross-output review. This route applies those aims to
performed moments. No cast size, protagonist, genre, theme, human anatomy, spoken language,
emotional arc, conflict, intimacy or conspicuous eccentricity is required. A conversation can
proceed before any image-generation setup.

## Prepared scene Persona material

During scene preparation, read the complete applicable Persona definitions, their portrayal
model, authorial intent and current conditions. Save the relevant definition text,
cross-definition dependencies, partner-specific exceptions and scoped applications with
[Scene Persona Material](scene-persona.md) as a record of understanding after reading; that
document also states when a reviewed bundle's `persona.md` is reused for execution, local repair
and resumption, and which changed conditions reopen the preparation. The material is authoring
input rather than canon or a performer's knowledge. Required text is saved in full; a size measure
says nothing about semantic completeness, and full reading and its scope review remain attributed
judgments.

## 1. Select a performance under a scoped identity

Individuality is a recognizable pattern or range of attention, interpretation, values, choices
and expression under authored conditions. Its center can lie in a steady presence, a
characteristic switch, a relationship among modes, a deliberate uncertainty or another chosen
pattern; a count of unusual traits or the distance from another subject measures something else.
Shared language can be intended.

Persona section 2 connects the internal core to the creator's intended portrayal; read it before
resolving a response. What a subject wants and what the creator wants an audience to experience
are different things. When several reactions would be plausible, the governing portrayal aim and
the protected dimensions select among them. Hesitation, volatility, a verbal tic, swagger,
vulnerability, trauma, dialects, flaws and contrasts each enter through the aim; variety alone
earns none of them, and a subtle unchanged response may carry the identity best.

A constant bearing through varied emotions and a usual lightness that switches to assurance
under selected conditions are different designs, and the same short line can realize either.
Both modes can be genuine, and calling one of them the real self is a design decision. Name the
signature dynamics, the permitted range and what would count as a meaningful departure. A
temporary contextual switch leaves the core and the persona phase as they were.

Where relevant, ground the pattern in specific concerns, activity and attention: what is noticed,
what merits explanation, when listening ends, what is enjoyed outside this plot. Reputation,
physique, species, gender, occupation and genre leave all of this open. A trait label is an index;
perform its actual choice, exact language, listening and movement, of which pronouns and endings
are one part.

Source-based work separates observation, self-report and inference; original work separates
anchors, proposals, adoption and hypothetical tests. Corroboration of a rule comes from outside
the sample written under it. Unsupported psychology and a guessed original creator intention stay
labeled as guesses. Read the persona's evidence boundary for real-person or record-based work.

## 2. Keep one owner for each kind of information

| Information | Owner and downstream use |
|---|---|
| Creator's portrayal aim, scope, recognition, contrast and departure policy | Authorial intent register in a design record; refer by stable intent ID and exact file/fragment. |
| Current identity profile, inner core, protected dimensions and signature dynamics | Persona section 2 PORTRAYAL IDENTITY, interpreting the governing intent without duplicating its rule/status. |
| World conventions, information access and physical possibility | Applicable world and adopted visual-identity records. A portrayal aim cannot silently change them. |
| Phase-wide body/voice baseline and behavioral repertoire | Persona PHYSICAL, MIND, THOUGHT, SPEECH and PERSONALITY, bound to section 2. |
| Systematic directional relationship exceptions | Persona RELATIONSHIPS, referring to the general rules and identity profile. |
| Current event, resources, fatigue, emotion, audience and task | Scene context or existing State Snapshot, not permanent identity. |
| This utterance, motion, observer access and timing | Scene realization or performance direction. Visual projection uses the existing performance contracts. |
| Adopted reusable visual-production summary | A bounded identity contract/sheet summary referring to applicable persona and intent owners. |
| Probe, inspected span, observations and remaining issues | Persona EXAMPLES/Portrayal Continuity Review or a scoped design review, not another rule owner. |

Within the persona:

- IDENTITY lists self and address forms and the compact scope.
- PHYSICAL owns stable vocal qualities and available channels.
- SPEECH owns linguistic and contextual delivery choices.
- THOUGHT owns appraisal and action procedures.
- RELATIONSHIPS owns directional exceptions.

Their rules reference section 2 and each other rather than repeat full definitions. Lasting
adopted change may need a new phase; an editorial correction is an edit rather than an event the
character experienced.

## 3. Establish baseline, envelope and binding

Describe a baseline in named conditions rather than as an average of incompatible modes. Several
recurring contexts, or an examined absence of a settled baseline, are both possible. A baseline
may be emotionally colored, private or rare in the output. Scope it to the intended identity.

Specify each channel:

- Speech: actual language or mode, address, register, clauses, lexical domains, precision, the
  order of answer, reason and request, questions, turn-taking, backchannels, silence and repair.
- Vocal delivery: habitual qualities, kept distinct from contextual tempo, volume, pitch movement,
  pauses, breath and articulation.
- Body: available channels, attention, posture, movement, contact and spacing.

Motor, sensory, cultural and environmental conditions constrain realization.

Bind each materially used baseline and mode to section 2, stating which identity dimension and
intent it realizes, what holds, what can vary and why, and which evidence or probe supports that
interpretation. A generic 'keeps their personality', or the same sentence copied into syntax,
listening and gesture fields, states none of that. The Core-to-Performance Bindings table is
navigation to the actual rule owners.

Maintain a permitted range rather than a script for every utterance. A person may change register
at work, alone, over text or with a trusted addressee, or deliberately keep it. Exact forms stay
in the actual language: a translation carries its own speech-level system, and translated wording
is evidence about the translation only.

## 4. Resolve conditional responses under that envelope

Use a relevant set of rules and resolve only the conditions that matter, keeping unknown or
undecided overlaps visible. A rule can branch, hold, return, linger or leave everything visible
unchanged.

For each consequential response connect:

1. **Identity and intent:** the applicable section 2 dimension and governing intent, with the
   intended continuity, contrast or authorized departure. A future plan activates nothing now.
2. **Situation and access:** the perceived event, task, available information, missed
   information, addressee and any other listener or observer. Solitude is a valid condition.
3. **Appraisal and purpose:** what it means to this subject and what they try to do or avoid,
   plus an established or proposed feeling where applicable. The appraisal, rather than an
   emotion label, is the cause.
4. **Initial control and expression:** intended display, habitual response, suppression, masking,
   ambiguity or an absence of change. The subject may only partly understand their own reaction.
5. **Held and changed components:** actual language, voice, listening and bodily changes, with
   owning mode and rule references. Protected dimensions hold, whatever expressiveness a change
   would add.
6. **Development, exit and bounds:** onset, switching, interruption, repair, release, re-entry,
   residue or an absence of return, where relevant, with conditions, limits, alternatives and
   actual basis.

For a contextual voice mode, state its activation, release, identity realization and the actual
linguistic, vocal and listening deltas. Affect, intent, addressee, audience, role, task, fatigue,
danger, medium and language matter only under relevant rules, and an emotional response may leave
some components untouched.

There is no universal rule that anger removes politeness, fear produces stammering, fatigue makes
speech short or affection removes distance. Professional code-switching, imitation and
accommodation are possible choices. A maintained formal register can carry warmth, anger or
routine.

### Overlap and switching during a conversation

Resolve conditions per component, not by a global ranking or a score. A trusted addressee may be
present before a formal audience, and an urgent task may coexist with courteous language. Compare the actual
authored rules, their scopes and their status. When a consequential conflict remains,
expose the gap or propose a scoped decision rather than inventing precedence. A local instruction
overrides identity or world facts only when it carries that authorization.

Record the switch point and any lag or repair. Address can change before rhythm; voice can settle
before bodily tension releases; emotion can outlast a new listener, and the body can stay tense
after the trigger ends. Track the mode across adjacent turns; re-entry conditions can differ from
entry. For a designed contrast, inspect whether the usual mode and the transition are actually
present in the relevant span; the mode's existence alone places the contrast in no scene.

## 5. Keep feeling, display, perception and inference separate

Distinguish four things:

- what is felt or proposed internally;
- what is intentionally shown;
- what is observably available now;
- what another subject or the audience infers.

These may agree or diverge, and leakage is a choice. A bodily cue is a suggestion; certification
of a hidden feeling comes from the author alone. An observer may misread a cue, notice only part
of it or miss it entirely. Narration and performance may withhold the cause.

Choose mechanisms and scene functions rather than an emotion-to-gesture lookup. Face, gaze,
posture, appendages, breath, motion, timing and distance may reinforce, oppose or lag each other,
and any of them can be absent or occluded. Environment and function may produce a similar
visible signal from a different cause. No expressive signal is mandatory.

Only declared anatomy, affordances and applicable conventions authorize a channel; ears, tails,
bilateral hands, facial muscles and eye-contact meanings each come from a declaration rather than
a category label. Signed language is language. Non-speaking subjects can use authored channels.
A machine's functional response is an operation, and an impersonal process stays impersonal even
when a persona form would prefer otherwise; it gets a functional and portrayal model of its own.

## 6. Resolve the moment, then select the medium

Before a consequential beat, settle a compact author-facing note covering:

- the applicable intent, its status and the identity profile;
- current facts and access;
- active rules and overlap;
- what must hold and what changes;
- actual voice and body timing;
- observers, outcome and uncertainty.

Distinguish an in-range realization from disclosure, disruption, lasting change and editorial
redesign. A meaningful departure rests on its authored scope, purpose, retained dimensions and
aftermath; an after-the-fact claim that anything is another side of the subject rests on nothing.

Each medium selects differently:

- Dialogue performs actual wording and interaction.
- Prose selects relevant actions and timing while keeping narrator diction distinct from
  character speech.
- Audio uses what can be heard.
- Comics and screen work stage sequences with spatial, task and prop continuity.
- A single image selects one instant and its visible channels, so it holds one reaction phase; a
  still can suggest duration while keeping its gestures compatible.

An unspecified intent is a gap to settle; displaying every cue compensates for nothing.

For visual records read [Performance Language Specification](../performance-language-specification.md).
It owns `performance_language`, `performance_cues` and prompt projection, and nothing about
dialogue modes. Retain authorial bindings in scoped notes rather than in invented JSON or service
fields. Apply retrieval and generation gates only on entering actual image work. Give the
downstream consumer (a writer, actor or renderer) a genuinely scoped view: remove hidden and
future material, including prohibitions that would reveal it, rather than copying the full
register with 'author-only' labels. Removal prevents leakage; a label alone leaves the knowledge
in place.

## 7. Review local realization and accumulated identity

A voice is demonstrated by a sample; a filled form is the sample's input. Use a manageable
relevant sample and retain the examined limits. Record inputs, held assumptions, observed
outcomes, counterexamples and revisions with their actual status. Hypothetical probes remain
hypothetical; story events are established separately.

- **Same context, relevant comparison:** hold facts and task steady. Compare actual attention,
  choice, exact language, listening and movement where distinction is intended. Similarity may
  be valid; a name-hidden comparison is diagnostic rather than a uniqueness quota.
- **Same subject, changed conditions:** vary a relevant condition, then a supported overlap.
  Inspect switches and held features. Explain how each realizes the identity, including minimal
  or unchanged responses. Solitary work is reviewed as solitary.
- **A new situation:** use an unused context within the declared scope. Record whether the
  portrayal fits, exposes a gap or needs a proposal. A rule revised only so the probe passes has
  passed nothing.
- **The actual span:** inspect ordinary and consequential moments together where they exist.
  Does constancy remain perceptible? Does intended contrast have space to function? Have small
  plausible choices cumulatively replaced the center? The serious-to-light ratio is whatever the
  design sets.
- **Departure and available channels:** when relevant, inspect the actual purpose, boundary and
  aftermath of a break, and expression under the requested medium. A dialogue task is reviewed as
  dialogue, a silent task as silence and a steady-character design as steadiness.

Treat repeated abstract field text, identical apologies, pronoun-only differences, paired generic
cues and tics in every line as possible problems to inspect rather than automatic failures.
Shared rules can be right, and a character may be intentionally difficult to identify. The
adopted aim sets the criterion; maximum difference is a different criterion. Compare
interpretation and world consistency as well as isolated line quality.

The read-only [Persona Expression Audit](../../scripts/persona_expression_audit.py), documented in
[Narrative Authoring](../narrative-authoring.md#inspect-expression-drafting-hygiene), reports
literal reuse and gaps. The authorial-intent audit checks explicit bindings and fingerprints.
Artistic quality, the meaning of a binding, approval, precedence and emotion stay outside both
audits. Run `python scripts/character_performance_smoke_test.py` for structural regressions. Read
[Character Performance Review Probes](character-performance-probes.md) for worked review
questions; the probes are examples rather than mandatory types or accepted persona designs.

## Revision and handoff

Revise the owning intent, profile or rules at the requested scope. Keep unrelated world,
character, theme and plot decisions fixed. Review affected sequences and refresh bounded sheet
summaries and projections. A transient register stays transient. The installed full templates
are the authoring source for new records.

Record reviewed hashes or versions, supplied consumer views and unresolved impact. Linked Markdown
edits do not invalidate JSON approval automatically; fingerprints show source content, and
consent and semantic adequacy are judged separately. State what was inspected; a long form or
zero linter findings proves only length and syntax.

## Ground the moment and inspect the handoff

For state-dependent portrayal, follow [World Realization](world-realization.md). Recover the
current persona phase, the independently resolved event state, known information and the exact
governing intent; a present voice mode derives from those, and the preceding output's visible
mood, a future revelation or a cached identity image is the wrong source. Keep onset, held
dimensions, switch, release and aftermath under their existing owners. The narrative state
resolver executes declared milestones, and that is its whole job.

An optional [portrayal principle](../portrayal-principles.md) can help articulate why constancy or
a switch carries identity. Read the complete entry, instantiate its actual rule in the persona and
bind its adoption in the design; each speech and body field gets its own concrete rule rather
than a pasted copy of the abstract definition. Review an unused condition as well as the
favorable example.

Check what the speaker can know separately from what an actor, writer or renderer may need.
Evaluate actual lines, vocal timing and available
body channels against both the current conditions and the intended identity; a structurally
correct world or a recognizable face shows something else.


## Artifact evidence and completion

For a saved deliverable, continue through [Production Execution](production-execution.md),
keeping this document's own interpretation, retrieval, approval and adoption boundaries:

1. Prepare the exact inputs.
2. Capture the real output.
3. Bind review and selection to it.
4. Complete and close the work task.

`scripts/production_workflow.py status`, `impact` and `resume` recheck dependencies and artifact
bytes. Completion and canonical adoption are explicit recorded steps; a progress checkbox, a
search hit or a newly created image is none of them.
