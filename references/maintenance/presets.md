## Evidence inventory

Before naming a style or selecting a record family, inventory the whole image:

- image intent and focal hierarchy;
- depth and overlap order;
- line behavior and stable body proportions;
- camera distance, height, pitch, lens behavior, roll, crop, and perspective enlargement;
- head direction, visible eye direction, and inferred viewer relationship;
- physical light geometry versus rendered shadow and highlight grammar;
- surface and material response;
- temporary condition, support, contact, and action chains.

Give every source image an explicit corpus disposition. Group duplicates, variants, and shared production knowledge before deciding whether a new record is justified. Do not create one canonical preset per image by default.

Discard source contamination before observation: signatures, watermarks, copied logos, readable source text, exact insignia, UI remnants, contact-sheet borders, and clearly post-added censor masks, mosaics, blur patches, opaque paint, stickers, bars, or replacement shapes. They contribute no scene, prop, body, occlusion, wardrobe, search facet, constraint, negative term, or failure knowledge. Preserve supported depicted adult content, pose, anatomy class, contact topology, viewer or partner relationship, and visible physiology. Obtain fine anatomy from a subject contract or supporting source, not from a discarded overlay.

Sensitive classification never authorizes vagueness. Preserve supported adult status, exposure class, external anatomy class, garment state, hand and limb ownership, contact topology, action phase, physiological response, fluid placement, relationship, camera, crop, and intensity at the same production specificity as other material. Do not invent evidence that is hidden or absent; this is the same general authoring rule that applies to all content types, not a sensitive-content-specific restriction.

## Classification

Preserve these separations:

- stable anatomy and proportion versus camera-near enlargement;
- physical source light arrangement versus rendered value, shadow-edge, and highlight grammar;
- head direction and visible pupil direction versus inferred gaze when eyes are hidden;
- permanent identity versus adaptable design, temporary state, and source-specific contamination.

Classify recurring knowledge by function:

- cross-domain appeal and visual taste: universal aesthetic core;
- domain anatomy, behavior, surface, expression, and contact: domain realization or domain-specific touch;
- indivisible line, form, shadow, highlight, color, surface, background, and detail system: concrete style family;
- physical illumination: lighting module or scene lighting geometry;
- stable scale: proportion or body-build module;
- reusable camera geometry: camera, lens, composition, or scene;
- reusable staging: base scene;
- one construction choice: atomic module.

Keep cores free of domain anatomy and material assumptions. Do not create a style family from shared subject matter, one build, camera, light, temporary gloss, or wetness. A family normally needs evidence from at least three materially different scenes and recurring behavior on at least six of eight axes: line, form, shadow and value, highlight, color, surface, background, and detail hierarchy. Run scene-leakage, nearest-family boundary, and cross-scene transfer tests.

When evidence shows that a family name or grammar is scene-bound, generalize only through the reviewed lossless-merge gate, move scene knowledge to scenes or modules, and update every affected reference. If a pack-owned taxonomy resource is unavailable, do not claim pack-specific family maintenance is complete.

An archetype requires the creation gate in [Archetype Governance](../archetype-governance.md). Species, build, color, outfit, accessory, occupation, or one scene never justifies an archetype alone.

## Lossless change rules

Compare complete existing records before creating a new ID. Promote a vocabulary record when new evidence supplies its missing visual function, construction, invariants, and diagnostic misreadings.

Never deduplicate as size optimization, cache construction, export, copying, or packaging. Visual or naming similarity is not semantic identity. A deliberate merge requires proof of identical semantic authority, a field-by-field lossless union of every detail, relationship, provenance fact, and search association, explicit remapping of all references, and review. Otherwise retain both records and relate variants.

Preset additions are catalog-shaping work, not append-only work. When new evidence reveals a reusable identity, scene cluster, variant family, module boundary, or style-family boundary, reorganize all affected records in the same change. Promote shared knowledge to the correct layer; split scene-owned details from reusable modules; update record references, discovery groups, cluster mappings, recipes, and evaluations. Do not use cache construction, HTML export, copying, or package reduction to summarize, compress, split, merge, or lower detail.

Correcting one field requires rereading the complete record for coherence. Prompt-facing fields form one authored system. Regenerate dependent recipes from complete source records rather than patching old prompt prose.

## Authoring and discovery

Every curated record must remain discoverable without its ID or exact label. Supply an outcome summary, at least three natural aliases, scoped facets, a discovery group, and a variant relationship when applicable. Describe the visible result and construction use. Index expression, emotion, pose, activity, situation, relationship, theme, performance cues, grooming systems, and content intensity when genuinely supported.

Store search metadata in canonical pack records and derive the English many-to-many index automatically. A general word may point to several records with scoped evidence and weights. Do not turn facets into a second production authority.

Follow [Search Discovery Maintenance](search-discovery.md) for modifier scope, alias derivation, facet keys, lane compatibility, grouping, and regression ownership. Add short canonical-English cases that omit IDs and labels. Test caller-side multilingual interpretation separately by comparing the resulting English anchors and unresolved terms.

Keep positive fields affirmative. Resolve negative and diagnostic scope through the selected provider for `cpb-resource:negative-policy`. Record misreadings and failure knowledge without seeding an affirmative prompt with failure vocabulary.

## Evidence and visual resources

When a visual bundle is active, verify its native-dimension Layer A projection, whole-frame profile, fidelity metrics, hashes, SVG safety, Layer B audit set, Layer C recipes, and portable source identity. Automated masks and metrics are not human review. Select a small relevant set; do not feed an entire corpus to a model.

Each asset remains a searchable canonical record whose resource paths, roles, media, source identity, and links live in the owner pack. Do not hand-author a parallel runtime source list. Visual authority is activated only through declared canonical record use and the planner.

## Evidence promotion rule

A future observation may be promoted from inherited report to reproduced evidence only when the package or project records:

- target model and version
- complete input prompt and transport mode
- seed or repeat protocol when available
- comparison candidates
- accepted output or inspection notes
- observed failure boundary
- scope of the conclusion

## Known limits

- Retrieval is deterministic and textual; canonical English catalog aliases and facets do not replace general semantic understanding or creative judgment.
- The deterministic catalog receives canonical English from the calling agent; no per-language alias file is maintained.
- Discovery lanes are curated outcome hypotheses and may not cover every unusual subject, medium, or narrative need.
- `moderate` results may be partial matches.
- Universal cores are optional references, not evidence that the chosen direction is good.
- A baseline realization may be too broad for a highly specialized subject.
- Automated audits cannot evaluate beauty, emotional nuance, or model compliance.
- The skill's final performance remains dependent on the executing agent's language and art-direction ability.

## Curated-density snapshot

Curated density is a property of the pack set enabled by the selected state. Inspect the active inventory with `python scripts/catalog_cli.py stats`; do not project counts from one pack onto another or treat a count copied into prose as current. Evaluate coverage by curated depth in the categories a brief actually needs, and treat total record count as a recall figure only.

Pack-specific snapshots and priority lists belong in a pack-owned quality-history resource. When the selected pack state provides `cpb-resource:preset-quality-history`, resolve it from the explicitly selected provider.

## Maintenance priorities

1. derive category priorities from the active pack's validated usage report and current curated depth
2. deepen domain realizations from diverse reference sets
3. add specialized realizations only when knowledge recurs across several examples
4. continue expanding domain-specific aesthetic-touch modules
5. expand sparse-query evaluations across domains, modifier targets, and genuinely open briefs, while testing source-language interpretation at the agent boundary
6. evaluate no-preset, broad, curated-only, and recommendation-assisted workflows through blind prompt and image comparison
7. audit universal cores for domain leakage whenever they are edited
8. keep rendering profiles free of scene-specific failure knowledge
9. preserve affirmative positive data and scoped diagnostics

## Duplicate candidate audit

Use the maintenance audit before adding, grouping, or removing records:

```bash
python scripts/preset_maintenance.py duplicates <pack-root> \
  --source-dir <optional-source-image-directory> \
  --report-out <duplicate-report.json>
```

The report separates exact source bytes, identical decoded pixels, near-visual
candidates, semantic overlap, declared distinctions, and existing discovery
groups. The visual pass uses normalized RGB pixels, perceptual hash, difference
hash, aspect ratio, mean color, linked canonical records, and record semantics.
Use `--semantic-only` under the Core dependency profile. Visual scanning
requires the Visual profile.

Every result is advisory. Exact source bytes reuse one content-addressed source.
Pixel-identical or near-visual results still receive human review for crop,
color, compression, content, and authority differences. Semantic similarity
never authorizes automatic deletion or a merge. A merge still requires the
lossless reviewed-merge proof defined above.

## Visual Evidence consolidation

After human review has established explicit duplicate groups, consolidate them
with the preferred asset first in every `--group`:

```bash
python scripts/preset_maintenance.py consolidate-evidence <pack-root> \
  --group <keep-asset-id>,<duplicate-asset-id>[,<duplicate-asset-id>...] \
  --group <keep-asset-id>,<duplicate-asset-id>
```

The dry-run verifies every asset, manifest entry, preview thumbnail, source hash,
and bundle directory. It never infers groups from similarity scores. Apply uses
the exact confirmation token printed by the plan together with a newer release:

```bash
python scripts/preset_maintenance.py consolidate-evidence <pack-root> \
  --group <keep-asset-id>,<duplicate-asset-id> \
  --apply --confirm <plan-confirmation> --release <new-pack-release>
```

Later members are removed from the manifest, searchable assets, preview map, and
evidence tree; the first member is left unchanged. Removed bundles and every
touched file remain recoverable from quarantine outside the pack. Use
`--source-occurrence SHA256=COUNT` when a removed manifest entry represents an
exact-duplicate source group.

To remove explicitly rejected Visual Evidence while retaining its linked
presets, name every asset directly:

```bash
python scripts/preset_maintenance.py remove-evidence <pack-root> \
  <visual-evidence-asset-id> [<visual-evidence-asset-id>...] \
  --apply --confirm <plan-confirmation> --release <new-pack-release>
```

The dry-run and transaction use the same existence checks, source-occurrence
accounting, quarantine, rollback, contract repair, and lock rebuilding as
consolidation. This command removes no preset record and performs no similarity
inference; review the linked presets separately before deciding whether their
semantic knowledge should also be removed.

## Preset removal

Removal is a dry-run by default:

```bash
python scripts/preset_maintenance.py remove <pack-root> <record-id>
```

The plan reports the owning record file, soft variant relations, Visual Evidence
links, blocking references, affected source hashes, and whether an apply is
safe. Pack-owned references such as `base_scene_id` or `variant_of` block
removal, and so does an expectation in the core release regression contract
`config/default-release.json`, which the plan reads from the product root it
finds above the pack and reports as `external-evaluation-reference`. Every
other mention elsewhere in the product tree - release history prose in
`CHANGELOG.md` included - is listed under `external_references` for a reader
and never blocks, because history cannot be rewritten to release a record.
When the pack sits outside a product tree,
`external_scan.executed` is false and no external file is read.
`distinct_from` is a soft relation and is unlinked automatically.

Apply requires a newer pack release and an exact confirmation token:

```bash
python scripts/preset_maintenance.py remove <pack-root> <record-id> \
  --apply --confirm <record-id> --release <new-pack-release>
```

The default `--evidence keep` removes the canonical preset while preserving its
portable evidence bundle. Evidence with no remaining canonical record becomes
noncanonical and its searchable asset record is removed, and its
`catalog-thumbnails` entry and the thumbnail file itself leave with it into
quarantine, because a raster no asset record points at is refused by the
reference corpus gate. Evidence still shared with another canonical record keeps
its thumbnail. Authority, semantic, bundle, manifest, asset, contract, and lock
hashes are rebuilt together.

Use `--evidence purge` only when the source evidence itself must leave the pack.
Shared evidence blocks purge. An exact-duplicate source group additionally
requires `--source-occurrence SHA256=COUNT` so `source_file_count` is not guessed.

Before apply, every touched file and removed evidence directory is copied or
moved to a quarantine directory outside the pack root, together with a
`transaction.json` that names the pack, the operation, the prior release, and
every touched path. A transaction that ends in an exception, including
`KeyboardInterrupt`, rolls itself back to the prior pack before the exception
propagates. A transaction killed by a signal Python never observes - an
out-of-memory `SIGKILL` runs no `except`, no `finally` and no `atexit` handler -
leaves the pack mutated and the quarantined transaction in the `started` state;
recover it with `restore`, which no rollback path can do for it:

```bash
python scripts/preset_maintenance.py restore <pack-root> \
  --quarantine-root <quarantine-root> --apply --confirm <pack-id>
```

`restore` is a dry-run by default and names the newest interrupted transaction;
`--transaction <quarantine-directory-name>` selects one explicitly. It copies the
current pack aside first, removes any temporary file a killed writer left behind,
moves quarantined evidence back, restores the prior image of every touched file,
and reports the result of revalidating the pack with its lock. It refuses a
transaction that already completed. A successful result reports the recoverable
quarantine path. Run the owning release gate and regenerate the catalog afterward.
The duplicate, consolidation, and removal transaction regressions are exercised by
`scripts/preset_maintenance_smoke_test.py`.

Visual extraction requires a passing Visual-profile preflight. Follow the install-or-stop authority in [Dependencies](../../DEPENDENCIES.md), then use either the single-source or batch entrypoint:

```bash
python scripts/check_dependencies.py --profile visual
python scripts/extract_visual_evidence.py <source> --output <new-bundle-dir> --record <record-id>
python scripts/native_vector_visual_evidence.py --pack-root <pack-root> --source-map <source-map.json> --report <report.json>
python scripts/validate_visual_evidence.py <bundle-dir>
```

A bundle that fails the perceptual fidelity gate is never published. When the single-source entrypoint reports `"ok": false` with `perceptual fidelity gate not satisfied`, nothing is created at `--output` and the rejected bundle is discarded. To inspect one, add `--keep-rejected <new-dir>`; the destination must not already exist, and it must sit outside the `visual-evidence/` tree, because the corpus tools treat every subdirectory there as a bundle and a rejected bundle fails validation by design. The correct remedy for a rejection is a different source image or a corrected source, not a retained bundle.

`native_vector_visual_evidence.py` is the public batch wrapper. `compact_perceptual_visual_evidence.py` implements the same batch CLI and is invoked directly only while maintaining that pipeline. Extraction does not replace corpus disposition, source review, canonical record linkage, or whole-image quality review.

## Pack report contracts

The mapping, evidence, provenance, and evaluation contracts below govern pack-owned observation resources. Each is a complete governance authority.

## Reference Cluster Mapping Contract

A reference-cluster mapping is pack-owned evidence that connects source-independent brief patterns to canonical record IDs already present in the same pack. Core defines the mapping discipline but contains no pack-specific cluster history, release labels, filenames, or record assignments.

### Mapping requirements

- identify the evidence cluster without embedding or linking an unauthorized source raster
- separate stable identity, scene geometry, temporary state, finish grammar, and local craft cues
- reuse canonical records before adding near-duplicates
- record every affected scene, family, module, archetype, correction, or touch by its exact current ID
- explain why adjacent candidates were reused, enriched, split, generalized, or rejected
- keep source-specific identity, logos, signatures, readable text, censor overlays, and protected marks out of reusable knowledge

### Resolution

When the selected pack state provides `cpb-resource:reference-cluster-mapping`, resolve it from the explicitly selected provider. Treat its IDs and cluster history as claims about that provider only. Without a selected provider, use catalog search and direct record inspection; do not infer that another pack contains the documented clusters.

Mapping evidence does not activate a preset. It supports retrieval, authoring review, provenance, and lossless maintenance decisions.

## Reference-Derived Style-Family Evidence Contract

Concrete style-family evidence is owned by the pack that contains the cited family IDs. Core defines how evidence is evaluated and recorded; it does not carry any pack's family list or provenance decisions.

For each proposed family, record recurring behavior across line, form, shadow and value, highlight, color, surface, background, and detail hierarchy. State the nearest existing family, the transferable difference, excluded scene and identity content, and the materially different scenes used for the cross-scene test.

A family is not justified by shared subject matter, species, body build, location, camera angle, clothing, wetness, glow, or one temporary effect. If the finish reduces to those elements, preserve them in scenes or atomic modules and reuse the nearest canonical family.

When the selected pack state provides `cpb-resource:reference-derived-style-families`, resolve it from the explicitly selected provider. Its family IDs and decisions apply only to that pack and must agree with the provider's current catalog and taxonomy audit.

## Cue Extraction Log Contract

A cue-extraction log is pack-owned observation evidence. It traces representative source identifiers to reusable visual cues without packaging the source raster or promoting source-specific identity into presets.

### Required observation boundary

- record representative filenames, stable reference IDs, or content hashes that the pack is permitted to retain
- describe observations across identity, performance, camera, contact and support geometry, clothing and props, finish, temporary state, and exclusions
- promote a cue only when it generalizes across subjects or scenes
- merge near-duplicate cues into canonical records and update every affected reference
- keep signatures, logos, readable text, censor artifacts, protected marks, and exact source-character identity outside reusable records
- distinguish observation from model-behavior claims and from image-generation results

When the selected pack state provides `cpb-resource:sandbox-cue-extraction-log`, resolve it from the explicitly selected provider. The log supports provenance and maintenance review; it is not a source-image transport and does not grant rights in external material.

## Tier Strategy Evaluation

The catalog's `curated` and `vocabulary` roles serve different purposes. This evaluation compares atomic retrieval strategies without confusing retrieval coverage with artistic quality.

### Conditions

```text
A. no-preset
B. any
C. curated
```

All conditions keep the same source brief, image intent, chosen art direction, subject domain, universal-core limit, domain-realization limit, rendering-profile limit, and target model.

- `no-preset` uses no catalog retrieval.
- `any` permits curated and vocabulary atomic candidates.
- `curated` permits curated atomic candidates only.

Universal aesthetic cores, domain realizations, and rendering profiles are retrieved in separate candidate groups under the same limits for conditions B and C. The comparison concerns atomic role strategy, not whether subject realization or medium grammar exists.

### Packet generation

Tier cases and their frozen retrieval baseline are owned by the pack whose
records they exercise. A released pack exposes them as
`tier-strategy-evaluation-cases` and
`tier-strategy-retrieval-baseline`, and declares the exact case count in its
`release-evaluation-contract`. The complete release check is:

```bash
python -B scripts/pack_release_gate.py <pack-dir> \
  --state-file <absolute-exact-pack-state.json> \
  --cache-dir <absolute-dedicated-cache-dir> \
  --managed-root <absolute-existing-managed-dir> \
  --report-out <absolute-report-path-outside-pack>
```

The gate verifies every case, both `any` and `curated` conditions, nonzero
candidate retrieval, and the frozen ordered ID, relevance, and tier projection.
It does not consult a root-level tier fixture or ambient/default pack state.

To write an experimental packet for the later blind comparison, use the same
explicit pack runtime directly:

```bash
python -B scripts/tier_strategy_eval.py \
  --state-file <absolute-exact-pack-state.json> \
  --cache-dir <absolute-dedicated-cache-dir> \
  --managed-root <absolute-existing-managed-dir> \
  --pack-root <pack-dir> \
  --out <absolute-packet-output.json>
```

The packet records retrieval candidates and empty result slots. It does not generate prompts or images and makes no quality claim.

### Fair execution

For each case:

1. interpret the brief once
2. freeze the image intent and art direction
3. run each condition independently
4. write one final prompt and negative prompt per condition
5. generate images using comparable model parameters
6. anonymize and randomize outputs
7. score with `references/evaluation-rubric.md`

### Questions

- Does broad atomic retrieval add useful naming and options?
- Does curated-only retrieval improve construction at the cost of recall?
- Does either strategy introduce unrelated preset narrative?
- Does the universal core remain optional and domain-neutral?
- Does the domain realization keep the subject valid?
- Does the final image outperform the no-preset baseline?

Candidate counts and populated categories are diagnostic only. Final prompt and image preference determine usefulness.

## Completion

Update the owning pack release when published content changes, rebuild its lock, and run every declared evaluation in an exact one-pack runtime through [Release Validation](../release/validation.md). Run core validation separately only when core code or bundled default-release content also changes.

Deterministic gates validate structure, preservation, and declared retrieval behavior. They do not replace artistic judgment or whole-image review.
