# FLUX Adapter

Use this adapter only for an active FLUX model or workflow record. Complete [Image Generation Runtime](../runtime/image-generation.md) first.

## Prompt rendition

Read the [Prompt Writing Guide Runtime](../runtime/prompt-writing-guide.md) before final rendition. Use its interface-neutral hierarchy, visible-scope, and controlled-comparison rules. Do not import Stable Diffusion bracket weighting, LoRA notation, or negative-field assumptions into a FLUX workflow unless the exact active record declares them.

Front-load subject identity and action, then composition, style, and context. Preserve explicit spatial and material relationships. Keep one visual grammar and avoid process vocabulary that would replace the selected finish with sketch, paper, brush, or strand artifacts.

## Negative transport

Do not assume negative conditioning exists in the active workflow. When the active record declares no negative conditioning, use `integrated-critical`: author affirmative construction for the activated technical requirements and retain the portable negative for traceability. When the workflow has an evidence-backed separate channel, use the exact transport declared by its model record rather than the family default.

## Reference forwarding

Prepare only media and counts declared by the active model record. Forward the verifier's effective prompt, exact negative channel, parameters, and committed reference carriers. Stop if the workflow cannot accept the complete reviewed plan.
