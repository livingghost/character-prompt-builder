# Video-model Identity Evaluation Battery

Run this battery only after a candidate target surface and an adopted reference bundle exist. Target capabilities must be documented or directly observed for the exact surface.

## Cases

1. neutral face close-up
2. three-quarter to profile head turn
3. full-body walk and weight shift
4. accessory occlusion and reveal
5. expression change with fixed camera
6. camera movement around a mostly still subject
7. start/end frame plus character-reference combination, when supported
8. two-clip continuation with identity re-anchor

## Measures

- signature-feature survival rate
- left/right consistency
- face same-ness
- marking stability
- body-proportion stability
- wardrobe topology
- accessory attachment
- flicker frequency
- style continuity
- endpoint adherence
- seam continuity

Preserve exact files, controls, text, settings, returned variants, and direct observations in the production record. A failure changes a target-scoped tactic; it does not prove a universal model limitation.
