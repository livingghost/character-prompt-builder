# Designed-switch realization probe

- **authorial_intent_refs**: [casual and focused modes](../design/teaching.md#intent-i-switch)

These are illustrative moments, not an adopted persona or established story.

Ordinary: "You came to help? Good. I was about to offer this thing a very polite argument."
The subject leaves space for an answer and talks while working.

Interruption: After hearing the obstruction, the subject ends the aside and directs attention
to the speaker. "Leave it with me. Tell me where it stopped." The address can remain informal;
the commitment and absence of joking carry the change.

Afterward: The subject confirms the task is safe to leave before the first small aside returns.
The ease returns without pretending the interruption never happened.

Review: the decisive line is the same as the other independent probe, but its surrounding
pattern differs. Making every line decisive would erase this aim. Do not infer that the earlier
lightness was false. Values, world capacities, precise timing and body channels remain to design.
