# Unpeopled realization probe

- **authorial_intent_refs**: [place framing](../design/teaching.md#intent-i-observation)

An open approach reveals an opening above a ledge. In the next view, intervening light makes the
opening harder to read; the ledge has not moved. Later, the opening is visible again from a changed
angle, but the realization does not name a mood or decide what the viewer should believe happened.

Review: the proposed variation is visibility and framing, not a spatial change. There is no
inferred personality, fear or comfort in the place. Check actual declared geometry when images
exist; this written probe cannot establish rendered fidelity. A supplied consumer view would omit
other entries in the author register, not ask a renderer to ignore them.
