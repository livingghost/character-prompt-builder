# Stable-presence realization probe

- **authorial_intent_refs**: [continuity of bearing](../design/teaching.md#intent-i-steadiness)

These are illustrative moments, not an adopted persona or established story.

Ordinary: Asked whether the task is ready, the subject finishes the current check and says,
"One more check." The response neither fills the silence nor pushes the questioner away.

Interruption: A new obstruction is named. "Leave it with me." The subject turns to the obstructed
work without an added display of panic or a newly ceremonial voice. The task may be difficult;
that does not by itself require a different presence.

Afterward: "That will hold. Check it before the next use." The acknowledgment remains concise.

Review: the important line has weight through continuity, not a sudden reveal of seriousness.
The subject need not repeat one pose or suppress every feeling. Actual persona values, task
knowledge and declared body channels must be resolved before this becomes a scene.
