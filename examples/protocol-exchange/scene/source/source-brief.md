# Mixed Viewpoint Workshop - Source Brief

Two recurring characters meet in a humid late-summer workshop. C01, a gray wolf mechanic, has a concealed left-side injury from an approved offscreen event and protects that side without revealing the bandage. C02, a russet fox archivist, holds a damaged silver pendant in the right hand and offers it to C01 for inspection.

The scene defaults to external character-aligned third person following C01. It uses a wide master, an aligned medium, an over-the-shoulder shot, one embodied first-person insert for the handoff, an external reaction close-up, and an external landing master.

The handoff is planned but remains non-canon because the example has not been run or accepted.
