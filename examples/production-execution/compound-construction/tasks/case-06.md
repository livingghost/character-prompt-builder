## Project brief

Create a landscape A5 printed comparison card for trainees learning to distinguish two woven patterns. The card should lead with the difference between the patterns, then let a reader inspect how individual threads cross. The title is "TEXTILE STUDY", and the two samples are labeled "A" and "B".

Each sample is square and contains twelve ivory horizontal threads crossing twelve navy vertical threads. The samples use the same thread scale. In A, each horizontal thread passes over one vertical thread and under the next, repeating across the sample; the next horizontal thread reverses that sequence. In B, each horizontal thread passes over two vertical threads and under the next two, repeating across the sample; each successive horizontal thread shifts that sequence by one vertical thread. These crossing patterns and counts are part of the design. The background is cream, and the lettering is navy. The arrangement, emphasis and decorative treatment remain open.
